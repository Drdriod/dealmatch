# DealMatch copy deck and tone

Source of truth for the words on the site. The deck below is the owner's approved copy. Where a page
appears in the deck, use its wording as written. Where a page is not in the deck, write in the same voice.

## Tone rules (for pages the deck does not cover)

- Plain, warm, confident. Short sentences. Talk to one person ("you"), not to "users".
- Use the deck's words: match, offer, "Find a Match", "Create an Offer", "Explore Places", "Explore People",
  "Protect Your Deal", "DealMatch Protection", "Verified", "Phone locked".
- Use "DealMatch Protection" for the payment and completion process. Avoid the word "escrow" in headlines and buttons.
- Promise only what the product does. Use "where eligible" or "eligible transactions" for protection. Never claim a
  verification, protection, refund or timing the code does not do. "Verified" appears only when the data says so.
- Calm, not hype. No exclamation marks, no slang, no "revolutionary", no "guaranteed".
- Buttons start with a verb: Find, Create, Explore, Save, Report, Continue, Try again.
- Empty states say what is missing and what to do next, in one or two sentences.
- Error messages say what went wrong in plain words and what to try. No codes, no blame.
- Brand lines: "Find It. Match It. Deal." and "Every deal starts with a match."

## Never change in a copy pass

- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy text, and any legal clause (changes need counsel).
- Numbers, fees, percentages, limits, dates, currency formatting, and any value from the database.
- Admin and staff screens, WhatsApp and Pidgin assistant messages, email templates, and notification text sent by the server.
- Code, props, ids, class names, routes, `data-*` attributes, analytics event names, and test hooks.
