# Uyo Market Strategy (diagnosis first)

Status: **Proposed**. Market claims below are hypotheses from the product and the founder's brief, not measured facts. No competitor or market-size data has been researched here; verify before relying on it.

## 1. Diagnosis

DealMatch is a trust-and-payment layer for property, hostels, hotels and service jobs. The real product is not listings (anyone can post those) but **verified people and protected money**. Where listings are free, the value is: the agent/landlord is real, the property exists, and the deposit is safe. That is the wedge.

## 2. Wedge for Uyo

Start with one narrow, frequent, painful case: **student and young-worker rentals and hostels near the university and city centre**, plus the **artisans those tenants hire** (plumbers, electricians). Reason: high repeat demand, small amounts, word of mouth is dense, and the same people are on both sides of the network.

## 3. Value equation

Value = (trust gained + time saved) / (fee + effort). For tenants: no fake listings, no lost deposit. For landlords/agents: verified demand, faster rent. For artisans: steady jobs and guaranteed payment. The fee must feel small against a scam avoided.

## 4. Network effects

Supply first (verified landlords and artisans), because tenants only come back if the listings are real. Referral rewards then reward the people who bring supply. Without supply density in one area, the referral programme just pays strangers.

## 5. Honest risks

- Escrow margin is thin or negative at 3% (audit C2): do not push escrow volume before fixing economics.
- Off-platform leakage: once two people meet, they can deal in cash. Protection and repeat benefit must outweigh the fee.
- Trust in a new payment brand takes months; the first real, successful, public escrow matters more than any feature.
- Referral cost can exceed fee if caps are loose; keep the 18% cap and monitor.
- Growth features (promoted listings, provider subscriptions, shareable profiles, admin redesign) before demand is shown are waste.

## 6. 30 / 60 / 90 days

| Window | Goal | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 | Fix money bugs; ₦100 real payment test; first live listing with lawyer sign-off; onboard 20 verified landlords/agents | Verified supply count |
| 31-60 | First 10 completed paid transactions (unlocks, jobs, hostel listings); collect real gateway fees | Paid transactions, cost per transaction |
| 61-90 | Repeat usage; referral from the first happy users; decide fee changes using real data | Repeat rate, referral share of signups |

## 7. KPIs

Verified supply count; searches that lead to an enquiry; enquiry to paid; repeat paid users in 30 days; gross revenue vs gateway cost; referral cost as % of fee; disputes per 100 transactions; time to resolve a dispute.

## 8. What to do before more code

Run the payment test, onboard supply by hand, and watch where people drop. Build only what removes a drop-off you can see.
