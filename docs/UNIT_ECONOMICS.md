## Flat-fee products

Subscriptions, hostel listings and unlock packs keep the whole amount; gateway cost is about 2% of the price plus VAT collected, so margin is about 98% before referral cost (max 18% of the fee).

## Break-even rule

Fee % must exceed gateway % (about 2%) plus payout cost plus referral/agent shares. With a 2% gateway, any fee below about 3% is not viable; any fee at 3% with shares paid out of it is not viable.
